DragonsBlock Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29 · Package: com.dragonsblock.app · Contact: support@dragonsblock.app

Overview

DragonsBlock ("we", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data may be processed when you use the mobile game.

Data we may process

CategoryPurposeProvider
Advertising ID (GAID / IDFA)Show ads, measure ad performance, fraud preventionGoogle AdMob
Purchase tokens / order IDsDeliver and restore in-app purchasesGoogle Play Billing
Local game progressHigh scores, settings, inventoryDevice storage only

We do not require an account and do not intentionally collect your name, email, postal address, or payment card numbers. Payments are handled by Google Play.

Ads

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may use device identifiers under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit ad personalization in device settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases go through Google Play. We only store entitlement flags on-device (for example, "no ads" unlocked). We never store card details.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game. Contact us if you believe we collected a child's personal data incorrectly.

Data retention

Local progress stays on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Ad and billing retention follows Google’s policies.

Your choices

  • Reset Advertising ID in Android settings
  • Disable personalized ads where the OS allows
  • Email support@dragonsblock.app for privacy questions

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do.
