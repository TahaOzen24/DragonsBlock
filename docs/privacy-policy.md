# DragonsBlock Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-29  
**Package:** `com.dragonsblock.app`  
**Contact:** support@dragonsblock.app

## Overview

DragonsBlock ("we", "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data may be processed when you use the mobile game.

## Data we may process

| Category | Purpose | Provider |
|----------|---------|----------|
| Advertising ID (GAID / IDFA) | Show ads, measure ad performance, fraud prevention | Google AdMob |
| Purchase tokens / order IDs | Deliver and restore in-app purchases | Google Play Billing |
| Local game progress | High scores, settings, inventory | Device storage only (SharedPreferences) |

We do **not** require an account. We do **not** intentionally collect your name, email, postal address, or payment card numbers. Payments are handled by Google Play.

## Ads

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may use device identifiers and similar data under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or limit ad personalization in device settings.

## In-app purchases

Purchases go through Google Play. We only store entitlement flags on-device (for example, "no ads" unlocked). We never store card details.

## Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game. If you believe we have collected personal data from a child incorrectly, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

## Data retention

Local progress stays on your device until you clear app data or uninstall. Ad and billing data retention follows Google’s policies.

## Your choices

- Reset Advertising ID in Android settings  
- Disable personalized ads where the OS allows  
- Contact us to ask privacy-related questions  

## Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
